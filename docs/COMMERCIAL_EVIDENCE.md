# Commercial Evidence Boundary

The commercial domain is engineering-ready but not commercially proven.

Activation, free-to-Pro conversion, trial-to-paid, paid retention, MRR, ARPU,
and churn are defined as metrics. Without real billing records the result is
`NO COMMERCIAL EVIDENCE`, not zero. Synthetic billing and demo events never
enter commercial metrics.

This repository makes no claim about revenue, payment success, conversion,
product-market fit, or willingness to pay. Owner decisions remain required for
price, terms, provider, billing operations, refunds, and deployment.
